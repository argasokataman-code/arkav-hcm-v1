# Tenant Reporting Template - Tax Governance

## Purpose

Standard template for tenant-side compliance and audit reporting in dual-domain tax governance:

1. Statutory payroll tax compliance.
2. Platform billing tax compliance.

## Reporting Frequency

1. Monthly operational report.
2. Quarterly compliance review.
3. Annual governance summary.

## Section A - Company Identity

1. Company name
2. Company code
3. Reporting month and period
4. Report owner and reviewer

## Section B - Policy Governance Lifecycle

1. Active policy UUID and version
2. Effective period start and end
3. Last submit/approve/publish timestamp
4. Last approver and publisher
5. Change history summary (what changed and why)

## Section C - Statutory Tax Compliance

1. Payroll runs in period
2. Payroll runs covered by active published policy
3. Uncovered runs and reason
4. Detected anomalies by severity
5. Resolved anomalies and evidence link
6. Open anomalies and mitigation owner

## Section D - Platform Billing Tax Compliance

1. Billing month
2. Billing tax policy UUID
3. Invoice count (paid/unpaid)
4. Total invoice amount
5. Applied tax rate
6. Computed tax due
7. Invoice-level exceptions and status

## Section E - Risk and Action Plan

1. Current risk level (green/yellow/red)
2. Top 3 risks
3. Mandatory remediations
4. Owner and due date per remediation

## Section F - Sign-off

1. Maker sign-off
2. Checker sign-off
3. Compliance reviewer sign-off
4. Timestamp and reference artifacts

## Evidence Attachment Checklist

1. Policy event history export
2. Anomaly register snapshot
3. Tenant self-audit export JSON
4. Billing invoice snapshot report
5. Supporting payroll/billing extracts
